Step 1 / 3 — MARKET KYA HAI
STEP 1

Market hai kya?

"Company ko growth ke liye capital chahiye; investor ko ownership ka exposure chahiye; market dono ko transparent process mein jodta hai."

Simple language me

Market wo jagah hai jahan ownership aur capital milte hain. Company shares bechkar paisa jama karti hai, investors hissa lete hain, aur secondary market me ye shares aapas me trade hote hain.

RBI kehta hai: capital markets economic development ke liye crucial hain — ye financial resources provide karte hain long-term sustainable growth ke liye.

Market me kaun kaun hai

  • Companies — capital raise karti hain
  • Investors — ownership lete hain
  • Exchanges — transparent platform dete hain
  • Brokers — access aur execution dete hain
  • Regulators (SEBI) — rules banate hain
  • Researchers — data aur analysis dete hain

Step 1 / 3 — MARKET KYA HAI (NEGATIVE)
STEP 1

Negative ko dekhna zaroori hai

SEBI ka data (FY22-FY24): 93% individual traders ko F&O me nuksaan hua. Aggregate losses ₹1.8 lakh crore se zyada.
SEBI ka naya study (2026): Options aur Futures me retail traders ki haalat aur bhi kharab — 9 din me ₹50,000 ka nuksaan average. Jitna zyada trade, utna zyada loss.
13 Aug 2026 — Sensex Expiry Manipulation: Do companies mil kar Sensex expiry ke din manipulation kar rahi thin. SEBI ne pakda. Ye dikhata hai ki market me fraud bhi hota hai — system hona zaroori hai taaki tumhare saath aisa na ho.

Asli wajah kyun log haarte hain

  • "Market" ka matlab sirf intraday/F&O/Telegram tips samajhte hain
  • Selective screenshots of winners dikhate hain
  • "Guaranteed return" ke claims karte hain
  • Hidden leverage use hota hai
  • Losses chhupaye jaate hain
  • Samjhe bina paisa lagate hain
  • System nahi hota — hwa me kaam karte hain

Sahi approach kya hai

  • Samjho — pehle market ko samjho
  • Negative dekho — 93% kyun haarte hain
  • System banao — rules, limits, record
  • Discipline — rules follow karo
  • Patience — results waqt leta hai
  • Grow karo system ke saath — system ke against nahi
Key insight: "Hwa me kuch nahi hota. System chal raha hai to koi model hoga — vo model continuity aur management ka hai. Jo log lagatar sikhta hai, aage badhta hai, system ke jaise dekhta hai — wohi abhi tak tike hain. Baaki sab hwa ho jaate hain."

Step 2 / 3 — SYSTEM KYA HAI
STEP 2

Hamara system kya hai?

"Hum brokerage, tip service, guaranteed-return service, ya live order desk nahi bana rahe. Hum pehle ek evidence workspace bana rahe hain."

System ka matlab kya hai

  • Rules pehle se likhe hain — jab entry/exit karna hai
  • Research continuous hai — naye ideas test hote rehte hain
  • Record honest hai — haar bhi dikhti hai, jeet bhi
  • Discipline hai — rules todna allowed nahi
  • Growth system ke saath hai — system ke against nahi

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

  • Research → Test → Verdict → Deploy/Reject
  • 35+ ideas test kiye — 30+ reject kiye
  • Har rejected idea ka record hai
  • Graveyard visible hai — sirf winners nahi dikhate
  • Dashboard pe sab live dikhta hai

Step 2 / 3 — SYSTEM KYA HAI
STEP 2

Safety Net — Kaise bache rehte hain

"Risk se bhaagna possible nahi, par risk ko naapna zaroori hai. Road risk ke liye seatbelt, rules aur driving skill chahiye. Market risk ke liye position limits, clean data, written rules, stop conditions aur separate capital chahiye."

🔴 Negative ko dekhte hain

  • 93% traders haarte hain — ye sabse pehle yaad rakhte hain
  • 13 Aug manipulation hua — ye bhi dekhte hain
  • SEBI ka naya study — retail aur bhi kamzor
  • Apne paper record me bhi loss hai — chhupate nahi
  • Edge÷cost 0.43× hai — abhi fees gross se zyada

Negative dekhna zaroori hai — par negative nahi hona. System isliye hai taaki negative ko samjhe aur uske saath play kare, bhaage nahi.

🟢 Safety mechanisms

  • Position limits — kitna risk lega, pehle set
  • Stop loss — loss 2% se zyada ho to exit
  • Written rules — baad me sochne ki zaroorat nahi
  • Separate capital — sirf wo paisa jo haar sakte ho
  • Paper first — pehle paper pe, phir real
  • Gate system — record weak ho to rukenge
Research = Business type mahol = Flow: "Jab research continuous hoti hai, to ek business type ka mahol banta hai. Usme flow banta hai. Market me tab jaake wo sakti hai. Aur har field me yahi hota hai — system chal raha hai to kaam hota hai, system nahi hai to hwa me jaata hai."

Kya diya jaayega

✅ Tumhe kya milega

  • Live dashboard — phone pe dekh sakta hai
  • Weekly updates — kya hua, kya nahi
  • Transparent record — haar bhi, jeet bhi
  • Written strategy — kya kar raha hai, kyun

❌ Kya nahi hai

  • "Guaranteed profit" — koi honest system nahi bol sakta

  • "Doubling scheme" — aisa kuch nahi
  • "Shortcut to rich" — evidence quality pehle
  • "Live buy/sell calls" — read-only evidence
Bottom line: "Humein ek aisa system banana hai jahan evidence hero ho — claim nahi. Agar record strong hua to next gate khulega; agar record weak hua to hum time par rukenge. Dono outcomes useful hain."
